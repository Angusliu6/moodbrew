# Mood / flavor compass (not a psychological or clinical instrument)

The user can choose **meet my mood** or **shift my mood**; personal preferences trump mappings. Present matches as poetic sensory associations, never scientifically established mood effects.

| Mood or desired experience | Suggested sensory direction | What to seek from photographed menu | Lower-caffeine alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| 静心/沉思/安静的专注 | rounded dark chocolate, toasted nuts, medium-heavy body, lingering mellow finish; mild acidity | medium/dark roast chocolate/nutty descriptors, natural sweetness, washed Colombian/Brazil-style blends when specified | half-caf or decaf with similar notes |
| 晴朗/清爽/自由 | citrus/floral, lively but balanced acidity, light body, clean finish | washed high-grown coffee with citrus/floral *when actually listed*; iced filter | decaf washed light roast if stocked |
| 温暖/被安慰/松弛 | soft texture, mellow perceived sweetness, low bitterness; milk optional | latte/flat white, nut/chocolate descriptors; oat/dairy choice | decaf latte |
| 开启行动/坚定/需要强烈仪式感 | deep roast notes, fuller body, assertive finish, or intense short espresso | espresso/dark roast/card with bittersweet notes | smaller size/half-caf |
| 探索/好奇/创造力 | distinctive fruits/florals, unfamiliar process, or contrasting region | natural, honey, anaerobic *if on menu*; ask barista what is newly featured | tasting sample / smaller pour |
| 平衡/稳定/日常 | moderate sweetness/acidity/bitterness/body, clear aftertaste | house espresso, approachable medium roast | decaf house option |

## Taste axis vocabulary (1–5, optional; avoid forcing a score)

- `sweetness`: perceived intrinsic sweetness, NOT whether sugar/syrup added.
- `acidity`: perceived brightness (citrus, apple, wine-like), not pH or defect.
- `bitterness`: roast/coffee bitter qualities; bitterness can be pleasant.
- `body`: mouthfeel weight/viscosity, not coffee strength or caffeine.
- `finish`: duration, 1 = short, 5 = long; **separate free-text character**: drying, chocolate, citrus, clean, etc.
- `after_sweetness` 回甘: later sweet impression after swallowing, not the same as initial sweetness or length of finish.
- `aroma`: perceived fragrance intensity; includes note descriptions.

## Decision heuristic, no false precision

If the user provides explicit 1–5 preferences, compare them narratively against **only known or described axes**. Do not invent numerical scores for beans from poetic labels. Recommend a safe familiar option plus "explore" choice. Confidence decreases if no processing or roast data. Ask user "你是在找与你现在同频的一杯，还是想换个心情？" if needed.

## Calibration, not manipulation

The same coffee may feel cozy or exciting to different people. Ask users after tasting whether the prediction fit, and update preference *in the diary* if permitted. The taste portrait is an evolving self-report summary, not fixed personality psychology.
